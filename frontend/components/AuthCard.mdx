# Auth card

A square panel centred on an otherwise empty page, holding a title, what
happened, and the one thing to do about it.

<Canvas of={AuthCardStories.Default} />

## Props

- `copy` — the namespace of the card's messages, such as `auth.pending`. The
  card reads its `title` and `body` from there.
- `children` — what to do next, at the foot. Optional.

## What the body may say

The body is rich text with a `<message>` part saying what happened, and an
optional `<suggestion>` part saying what to do about it, set smaller.

<Canvas of={AuthCardStories.WithASuggestion} />

A body with nothing to suggest is the one line, and nothing under it.

<Canvas of={AuthCardStories.WithoutASuggestion} />

## Not only for signing in

A page that is not there has the same shape, so it is the same card with
different messages.

<Canvas of={AuthCardStories.APageThatIsNotThere} />

## Without an action

Nothing to do next leaves the foot empty rather than changing the layout.
