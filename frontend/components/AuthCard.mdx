# Auth card

A square panel centred on an otherwise empty page, holding a title, what
happened, and the one thing to do about it.

<Canvas of={AuthCardStories.Default} />

## Props

- `title` — the page's `h1`, at the top of the card.
- `children` — what happened. Convention is a `text-lg` line saying it, and an
  optional `text-sm` line saying what it means for the reader.
- `action` — what to do next, at the foot. Optional.

## A second line

The card holds its shape whether the body is one line or two: the title stays
at the top, the action at the foot, and the body between them.

<Canvas of={AuthCardStories.WithASecondLine} />

## Without an action

Nothing to do next leaves the foot empty rather than changing the layout.
