# Auth card

A square panel centred on an otherwise empty page, holding a title, what
happened, and the one thing to do about it.

<Canvas of={AuthCardStories.Default} />

## Props

- `copy` — names the card's copy in the catalogue, whole: `auth.pending`. The
  card reads `title` and `body` from there rather than being handed them, so
  the wording of a page lives with the rest of the wording.
- `children` — what to do next, at the foot. Optional.

## What the body may say

The body is rich text, and the message decides which parts it has: a
`<message>` saying what happened, and an optional `<suggestion>` saying what to
do about it. The card decides how each one reads — the suggestion is the
smaller, quieter line.

<Canvas of={AuthCardStories.WithASuggestion} />

A body with nothing to suggest is the one line, and nothing under it.
