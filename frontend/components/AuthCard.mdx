# Auth card

The shape the three auth pages share: a square panel centred on an otherwise
empty page, holding a title, what happened, and the one thing to do about it.

<Canvas of={AuthCardStories.Default} />

## Props

- `title` — the page's `h1`, at the top of the card.
- `children` — what happened. Convention is a `text-lg` line saying it, and an
  optional `text-sm` line saying what it means for the reader.
- `action` — what to do next, at the foot. Optional: some errors leave nothing
  to try.

## One card, three moments

Signing in, being turned away, and waiting for a role are the same moment seen
three ways — so they are one shape. Someone who meets the second having just
seen the first should not have to work out that they are still in the same
place.
