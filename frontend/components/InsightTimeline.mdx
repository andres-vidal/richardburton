# Insight timeline

A titled list of rows on one axis of years. Each row shows its label and value,
and a track with a dot at the year of each mark, joined by a line from the
first mark to the last.

<Canvas of={InsightTimelineStories.Weighted} />

## Props

- `title` — the heading of the chart, and the accessible name of the section
  around it.
- `hint` — an optional explanation of what the chart shows, in an
  [Info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs) beside the title.
- `tracks` — the rows, each with a `key`, a `label`, an optional `detail` shown
  after the label, a `value`, which is the row's count as text, and its
  `marks`.

Each mark has a `year`, a `label` that says what the dot stands for, and an
optional `weight`. A dot's size is its weight relative to the largest weight in
the chart.

## Dots without a weight

When the marks have no weight, every dot is the same size, a third of the way
between the smallest and the largest.

<Canvas of={InsightTimelineStories.Unweighted} />

## The axis

The axis runs from the decade of the earliest mark to the end of the decade of
the latest, the same for every row, so rows can be compared. Decades divisible
by 20 are always labelled, and the others only on screens at least as wide as
the `md` breakpoint. The labels at either end are aligned inwards, so they stay
inside the axis. On narrow screens, each row's label is written above its
track.

## Hover

Hovering a dot shows its mark's label, such as the year and translators of a
translation.

## Accessibility

The tracks and the axis have `aria-hidden`. Each row is a list item that reads
its label and value, and then the labels of its marks.
