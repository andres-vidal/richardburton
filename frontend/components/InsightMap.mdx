# Insight map

A titled world map with each country shaded by its number of publications, and
a list of the countries with their counts beside it. On narrow screens the list
is below the map.

<Canvas of={InsightMapStories.Countries} />

## Props

- `title` and `hint` — see [Insight title](?path=/docs/insights-insight-title--docs).
- `map` — the outlines to draw, from `worldMap` in `modules/world-map`. It
  projects Natural Earth's outlines, from the `world-atlas` package, and is
  meant to be computed on the server, so the outline data and the projection
  library are not part of the page's JavaScript.
- `countries` — every country with publications, with its code, name and
  count, in the order they are listed.

## Shades

The shades are fixed bands rather than a scale of the largest count, so a shade
means the same range on every search: one publication, two to nine, ten to 99,
and 100 or more. A legend under the map names the bands. Countries without
publications are grey, and so are territories the atlas draws without a
country code.

## Hover and accessibility

Hovering a shaded country darkens it by one shade and shows its name and count,
from the shape's SVG `title`. The map has `aria-hidden`, and assistive technology reads
the list beside it, which has every count.
