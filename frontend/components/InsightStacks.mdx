# Insight stacks

A titled list of stacked bars, one for each entry, such as each decade. Each
row shows its label and total above a grey track, and a bar on the track stacks
the row's series from the left. A bar's length is the row's total as a fraction
of the largest total, so a row with a total of 0, such as a decade with no
publications, shows only the track.

<Canvas of={InsightStacksStories.Decades} />

## Props

- `title` — the heading of the chart, and the accessible name of the section
  around it and of its table.
- `hint` — an optional explanation of what the chart shows, in an
  [Info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs) beside the title.
- `heading` — the heading of the table's first column, such as "Decade".
- `series` — the name of each series, in the order they are stacked from the
  left. They are listed in an [Insight legend](?path=/docs/insights-insight-legend--docs).
- `rows` — the entries, each with a `key`, a `label` and its `counts` in the
  order of `series`.

## Hover

Hovering a row shows its count in each series. The play test of the first
story checks the text it shows for the 1960s.

## Accessibility

The bars have `aria-hidden`. The section holds an
[Insight table](?path=/docs/insights-insight-table--docs) of the same counts,
which assistive technology reads instead.
