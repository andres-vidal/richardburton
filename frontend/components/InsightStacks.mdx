# Insight stacks

A list of stacked bars, one for each entry, such as each decade. Each row shows
its label and total above a grey track, and a bar on the track stacks the row's
series from the left. A bar's length is the sum of the row's counts as a
fraction of the largest sum, so a row with no counts, such as a decade with no
publications, shows only the track.

<Canvas of={InsightStacksStories.Decades} />

## Props

- `title` — the caption of the table of counts, which is the title of the
  chart, and so the table's accessible name.
- `heading` — the heading of the table's first column, such as "Decade".
- `series` — the name of each series, in the order they are stacked from the
  left. They are listed in an [Insight legend](?path=/docs/insights-insight-legend--docs).
- `rows` — the entries, each with a `key`, a `label`, an optional `detail`, and
  its `counts` in the order of `series`.
- `shares` — whether each bar shows the share of each series in its row.

## Hover

Hovering a row shows its count in each series, with its share when `shares` is
set. The play test of the first story checks the text it shows for the 1960s.

## Accessibility

The bars have `aria-hidden`. The component also renders an
[Insight table](?path=/docs/insights-insight-table--docs) of the same counts,
visually hidden, which assistive technology reads instead.
