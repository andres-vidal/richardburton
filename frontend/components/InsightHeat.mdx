# Insight heat

A titled table with a row for each group, such as an author, and a column for
every decade from the earliest cell to the latest, drawn in an
[Insight section](?path=/docs/insights-insight-section--docs). Each cell shows
its count and is shaded by it.

<Canvas of={InsightHeatStories.AuthorsByDecade} />

## Props

- `title` — the heading of the table, and the accessible name of the section
  around it and of the table.
- `hint` — an optional explanation of what the table shows, in an
  [Info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs) beside the title.
- `actions` — optional controls shown at the end of the title's row, such
  as a chart's settings.
- `heading` — the heading of the first column, such as "Original author".
- `rows` — the groups, each with a `key`, a `label`, an optional `detail`, and
  its `cells`. Each cell has `at`, the first year of its decade, its `total`,
  and a `label` that says what it counts.

## Shades

The shades are the four bands of the map's `data-shade`, but relative to the
largest count in the table rather than fixed, since a cell's counts are much
smaller than a country's. A decade with no count is left blank.

## Hover and accessibility

Hovering a cell shows its label in the browser's tooltip, from the cell's
`title`. The table scrolls sideways on a narrow screen, so a tooltip drawn
inside it would be cut off. Assistive technology reads it as a table, so it
needs no other text in its place.
