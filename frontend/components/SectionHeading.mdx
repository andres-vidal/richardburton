# Section heading

What a part of a page or a dialog is called. Small, uppercase and grey, so a
page of several sections reads as one thing rather than as a stack of headings
competing with the content under them.

<Canvas of={SectionHeadingStories.Default} />

## Props

`children` — the heading text. Renders an `h2`, which is what a section under a
page's `h1` is.

## The class list on its own

`SECTION_HEADING` is exported for the places where the heading is not an `h2` —
a `<summary>` that opens a collapsible section, for one, which has to stay a
`summary` to keep its behaviour.
