# Section heading

The name of a section of a page or a dialog, in small, grey capitals, so it
stays quieter than the content under it.

<Canvas of={SectionHeadingStories.Default} />

## Props

`children` — the heading text. Renders an `h2`, which is what a section under a
page's `h1` is.

## The class list on its own

`SECTION_HEADING` holds the heading's classes, for a heading that cannot be an
`h2`, such as the `<summary>` of a collapsible section.
