# Notification

One message about something that just happened. Purely presentational: it knows
nothing about where it sits, how long it lives, or what raised it —
[`Notifications`](?path=/docs/components-notifications--docs) owns all of that.
Kept separate so the card can be read, reviewed and audited on its own, without
a portal and a four-second timer in the way.

<Canvas of={NotificationStories.Default} />

## Writing one

Two lines, each with a job. The **message** names what happened, in the past
tense and without an apology — "Publication deleted", not "Successfully deleted
the publication!". The **detail** is optional and carries what the message
cannot: which record it applies to, or what to do next. A failure without a
detail tells the reader they are stuck; a failure with one tells them the way
out.

<Canvas of={NotificationStories.Failure} />

## Levels

`success` and `info` are confirmations and announce politely (`role="status"`).
`warning` and `error` are failures and interrupt (`role="alert"`), because they
usually need acting on. The card derives that from its level through the
exported `isFailure`, which the container also reads to decide what times out —
so the two can never disagree about which levels are failures.

The level shows as a coloured edge rather than an icon, in the same semantic
palette the [history](?path=/docs/publications-publication-history--docs) uses
for its actions, so red means the same thing everywhere in the app.

<Canvas of={NotificationStories.Levels} />

## Dismissing

`onDismiss` renders the close button and is called on click; the card does not
remove itself. Omit it for a card that cannot be dismissed — the container uses
that for its "N more notifications" summary.
