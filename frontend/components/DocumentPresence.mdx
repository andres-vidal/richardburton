# Document presence

Who else has this document open right now, each as their initial in their own
colour. It sits in the workspace header beside the document's status.

<Canvas of={DocumentPresenceStories.OthersHere} />

## Who is here, not who may be

The list of documents is shared, so this says nothing about who is allowed in.
The server keeps it, per connection and under the address it holds for each
person, so nobody is shown on their own say-so and nobody lingers after their
tab has gone. It is true only while somebody is looking, and is never written
down.

## One person, however many tabs

The server counts connections, and one person may have the document open in
several tabs. The list folds them together by the address they signed in with,
so one person reading in two tabs is one face, not two.

<Canvas of={DocumentPresenceStories.OnePersonInTwoTabs} />

## Props

None. It reads who is here from the document `LiveProvider` supplies, and says
nothing where nobody else is.
