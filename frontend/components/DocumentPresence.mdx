# Document presence

Shows the other people who have this document open, each as their initial in
their presence colour. It sits in the workspace header, next to
`DocumentStatus`.

<Canvas of={DocumentPresenceStories.OthersHere} />

## Who is listed

Anyone who may edit publications can open any document, so this list shows who
has the document open now, not who is allowed to. The server keeps the list
with Phoenix Presence. It tracks each connection under the email of its
signed-in user, so a client cannot choose the name it is listed under. A person
leaves the list when their tab closes, and the list is not stored.

## One entry per person

The server tracks connections, and one person may have the document open in
several tabs. `useOthersPresent` returns one email per person, so a person with
two tabs open is shown once.

<Canvas of={DocumentPresenceStories.OnePersonInTwoTabs} />

## Props

None. It reads the people from the `LiveProvider` context with
`useOthersPresent`. It renders nothing when nobody else has the document open.
