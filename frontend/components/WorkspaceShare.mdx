# Workspace share

Who may open this workspace, who is looking at it right now, and how to let
somebody else in. It sits in the workspace footer, and renders nothing at all on
a surface that belongs to no workspace — `/admin/publications/new` is the same
screen without one.

## Membership and presence are different things

**Membership** is what the server keeps. It decides who may open the workspace
at all, and it outlives everyone closing the tab. Only the owner may add to it,
and only by an address someone has actually signed in with — there is no
invitation flow here, so an address nobody has used is refused rather than left
pending.

**Presence** is what awareness carries over the live connection. It is true only
while someone has the workspace open, is never written down, and disappears when
they leave. That is why the two are shown differently: members as a list in the
dialog, presence as initials that come and go.

## Props

- `invite` — how someone is let in. Defaults to asking the server.
- `read` — how the members are read. Defaults to asking the server.

Both are props so a story can stand in for the network.

## States

- **Default** — the members, and the field to add another.
- **Nobody by that address** — membership is by an account that exists, so an
  unknown address says so.
- **Someone else is here** — another person has the workspace open.
- **No workspace** — nothing to share, so no control.
