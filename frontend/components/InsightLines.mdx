# Insight lines

A titled line chart over years or decades, drawn in an
[Insight section](?path=/docs/insights-insight-section--docs), with one line
for each series. The largest value drawn is written at the top of the chart.

<Canvas of={InsightLinesStories.Lines} />

## Props

- `title` — the heading of the chart, and the accessible name of the section
  around it and of its table.
- `hint` — an optional explanation of what the chart shows, in an
  [Info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs) beside the title.
- `actions` — optional controls shown at the end of the title's row, such
  as a chart's settings.
- `heading` — the heading of the table's first column, such as "Year".
- `series` — the name of each series. With more than one, they are listed in an
  [Insight legend](?path=/docs/insights-insight-legend--docs).
- `points` — every year or decade to draw, in order, with the same fields as
  the columns of [Insight columns](?path=/docs/insights-insight-columns--docs).
- `stacked` — whether the series are stacked as areas rather than drawn as
  lines.

The axis is labelled like the columns chart: years divisible by 20, and the
other decades on wider screens.

## A running total

The chart draws the counts it is given, so a running total is drawn from
counts that `cumulative` in `modules/chart` has added up.

## Hover

Each point has a column the full height of the chart. Hovering it draws a
vertical line through the point and a dot where it meets each series, and shows
the point's label, its total and its count in each series.

## Accessibility

The drawing has `aria-hidden`. The section holds an
[Insight table](?path=/docs/insights-insight-table--docs) of the same counts,
which assistive technology reads instead.
