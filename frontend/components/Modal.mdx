# Modal

A dialog over a dimmed overlay, rendered in a portal while `isOpen`. It calls
`onClose` on <kbd>Esc</kbd> and on a press on the overlay outside the dialog,
keeps focus inside the dialog, and locks the page's scroll. On a small screen
the dialog fills the screen, under a bar with the app's name and a close button.

When the modal opens, focus moves to the overlay that holds the dialog, so the
keyboard starts inside the modal. The overlay draws no focus ring.

## Props

- `isOpen` — whether the dialog is shown.
- `onClose` — called when the reader asks to close it.
- `heading` — what the dialog is called. The modal renders it as the dialog's
  `h1` in a [Modal heading](?path=/docs/layout-modal-heading--docs) bar, and
  the dialog takes its accessible name from it.
- `subheading` and `aside` — a line under the heading, and a short text at the
  end of its bar, such as a position in a queue.
- `label` — the accessible name. Required without a `heading`. With one, it
  names the dialog instead of the heading.

## With a heading

The heading sits at the top of a padded body and stays pinned while the content
scrolls under it. The children follow it as the items of a column, 1.5rem apart,
so a caller passes its paragraphs, sections and buttons as they are.

## Without a heading

The children fill the dialog as they are, so they bring their own padding and
heading, such as an [Article](?path=/docs/components-article--docs), and `label`
names the dialog.

## Popovers inside the dialog

The modal is a node of a floating tree (`FloatingTree` in `@floating-ui/react`),
and starts one when it is not inside one. A popover opened from the dialog's
content that registers as a child node, with `useFloatingNodeId` and
`FloatingNode`, is part of the dialog even though its portal is outside it:
focus may move into it, and it is not hidden with the rest of the page.

## Escape

<kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the modal through `useDismiss` from `@floating-ui/react`,
and only when nothing inside the modal takes the key press first.

- A child node of the modal's floating tree that is open, such as a modal
  opened from this one's content, closes on <kbd>Esc</kbd> instead of this
  modal.
- A floating element that closes on <kbd>Esc</kbd>, such as the menu of a
  [select](?path=/docs/components-select--docs) or the link dialog of a
  [rich text area](?path=/docs/components-rich-text-area--docs), stops the key
  press, so the modal does not receive it.
- While a key is being composed, as with an input method or a dead key, the
  modal ignores <kbd>Esc</kbd>.

## Hooks

- `useModal()` keeps the open state in component state. It returns `isOpen`,
  `open` and `close`.
- `useURLQueryModal(param)` keeps it in the URL. The modal is open while the
  `param` query parameter has a value, so a link such as `?contact=true` opens
  it. `open` and `close` replace the current history entry, keep the rest of the
  query and leave the scroll position alone.
