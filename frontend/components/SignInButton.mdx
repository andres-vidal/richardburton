# Sign in button

An outline [Button](?path=/docs/components-button--docs) with the Google logo
that starts signing in with Google. It loads the `/api/auth/google` route
handler as a whole page, because the handler answers with a redirect to Google
that the client router cannot follow.

<Description of={SignInButtonStories.Default} />
<Canvas of={SignInButtonStories.Default} />

## Props

- `label` — replaces the plain offer to sign in, for a page that says why it
  asks.
- `centered` — centres the icon and label in the button instead of aligning
  them left.
