# Chart builder

The chart builder of the insights page: a toolbar of the controls that say what
a chart shows, and right under it the chart itself, drawn by an
[Insight chart](?path=/docs/insights-insight-chart--docs), with the controls
that refine it in its title row.

<Canvas of={ChartBuilderStories.Controls} />

## Props

- `description` — the chart the page's address described when the server
  rendered the page, as `parseDescription` in `modules/chart` reads it.
- `drawn` — its counts, from `GET /insights`.

## The toolbar

The toolbar has five [Selects](?path=/docs/components-select--docs): the
preset, what to count, by what, split by what, and in which form. Their options
name themselves, such as "by decade" or "as columns", so the toolbar needs no
labels on screen, and each Select has its name as its accessible label.

The presets are the questions the insights page answers by default:
publications per year and per decade, the most translated authors, the authors
first translated in each decade, the leading translators, author–translator
pairs and publishers, the works translated more than once, and the countries of
publication. The preset Select shows the preset the chart is, or "Custom" when
it is none, and choosing a preset sets every control to its chart.

header while the chart scrolls under it.

## What each count can be grouped by

A year, a decade, a country and a kind belong to a publication. So publications
take every grouping, split and form, and the other counts are limited:

| Count            | Placed in time      | Grouped by                             |
| ---------------- | ------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Works            | at their first year | original author, translator, publisher |
| Translations     | at their first year | work, original author, translator      |
| Original authors | at their first year | translator, publisher                  |
| Translators      | at their first year | original author, work                  |
| Publishers       | at their first year | —                                      |

An item's first year is the year of its earliest publication in the database,
so these counts are counted at first appearances whenever they are placed in
time, in a time grouping, a timeline or a heat table. They take no split, so
shares are not offered for them either. Changing the count to one the grouping
does not fit groups it by year.

## The chart's title row

The title row has how many groups to keep and how many items each needs. They
apply to ranked groups, except on a map, which shows every country, and they are
disabled otherwise, so they keep their places.

not apply, so changing one control does not move the others.

## The address

Every change goes through `normalize` and is written to the page's address,
keeping the search. The play tests check the address each change writes.

<Description of={ChartBuilderStories.KeepingTheSearch} />

<Canvas of={ChartBuilderStories.KeepingTheSearch} />

Most changes ask `GET /insights` for other counts. The builder replaces the
address with `router.replace` in a transition, and the page reads the new chart
on the server. The controls show the new choice at once, and the current chart
stays on screen, dimmed, until the new one arrives.

Some changes ask for the same counts, as `chartParams` in `modules/chart`
writes them, such as a switch between columns, lines, stacked areas and
running totals of the same groups. The builder draws such a change
from the counts it has, and writes the address with `ADDRESS.replace` from
`modules/address`, which calls `window.history.replaceState`. Next.js updates
`useSearchParams` and `usePathname` to the new address without asking the
server for the page. The stories replace `ADDRESS.replace` with a mock, so the
story frame keeps its own address.

A change made while another is still pending goes through the server too.
Otherwise the pending chart would replace the one drawn without it when it
arrives.
