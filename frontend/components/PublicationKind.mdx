# Publication kind

One line that says a publication is a retranslation or a reissue. It shows a
badge that names the kind, an [info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs)
that defines the kinds, and a sentence that links to each earlier publication
this one follows. It renders nothing for a first translation.

The server decides the kind of a publication and the publications it follows,
and `GET /publications/:id` returns both with the publication. It compares
publications by year only, so publications of the same year count as published
at the same time. A publication of the year the work was first translated is a
first translation. A publication of the first year of a later translation of
the work is a retranslation. A publication of a later year than its
translation's first year is a reissue. The hint shows the same definitions as
the insights charts.

A retranslation follows every publication of the year the work was first
translated, and a reissue every publication of its translation's first year.
With one, the sentence names its title and year. With several, the sentence
names their year once, and then each one's title with its publishers and
countries, which tell apart publications of the same title.

## Props

- `kind` — `"first_translation"`, `"retranslation"` or `"reissue"`.
- `follows` — the publications this one follows, each with its id, title,
  year, translators, publishers and country codes. It is empty for a first
  translation.
