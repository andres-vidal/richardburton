# Cell presence

Marks a cell that another person has focused. It shows their initial in their
presence colour, in the cell's top-right corner. Hovering the initial shows
their email.

<Canvas of={CellPresenceStories.Default} />

## Why an initial as well as a colour

A colour alone shows that somebody is in the cell, but not who. The initial
shows who. The colour is the same one the person has in `DocumentPresence` in
the page header, so a cell can be matched to a person without hovering.

The cell also draws a bar in the same colour along its left edge. The bar is
part of the cell's styling, not of this component.

## Props

- `colour` — the index of the person's presence colour, from `colourOf` in
  `modules/publication/presence`.
- `by` — the person's email address.
