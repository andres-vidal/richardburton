# Cell presence

Somebody else's cursor, in the cell they have it in: the initial of whoever it
belongs to, in their colour, in the cell's corner. Hovering names them.

<Canvas of={CellPresenceStories.Default} />

## Why an initial as well as a colour

A colour alone says that somebody is there without saying who. The initial says
who, and the colour is the same one that person carries in the list of who is
here at the top of the document, so a cell can be traced to a person without
hovering at all.

The cell also draws a bar in the same colour along its edge; that is the cell's
own styling, not this component's.

## Props

- `colour` — which of the palette's colours this person is drawn in, from
  `colourOf` in `modules/publication/presence`.
- `by` — the address of whoever it is.
