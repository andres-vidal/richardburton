# Insights

Counts for every publication in the database, or for the ones a search
matches. `Insights` is the content of the insights page at `/insights`, and
`InsightsHeading` is the page's subheader.

<Canvas of={InsightsStories.Everything} />

## Props

Both take a single `insights` prop, the response of `GET /insights`. Most of
its counts are numbers of publications, so a book published twice counts
twice.

- **Figures** — see [Insight figures](?path=/docs/insights-insight-figures--docs).
- **Publications by decade** — every decade from the first to the last,
  including decades with no publications.
- **Most translated authors**, **translators** and **publishers** — up to ten
  names in each field with the most publications.
- **Countries of publication** — every country, named in the reader's language.
- **Works translated more than once** — a work is an original book, and a
  translation is one translated text of it by its translators. This list
  counts translations, so a translation published twice counts once.

## Empty lists and empty results

The list of works translated more than once is left out when no work has more
than one translation. The other lists always have items when there are
publications.

<Canvas of={InsightsStories.NoWorkTranslatedTwice} />

When a search matches no publications, the component shows a message instead
of the figures and lists.

<Canvas of={InsightsStories.NothingMatched} />

## InsightsHeading

`InsightsHeading` shows how many publications are counted, the links to the two
views from `DatabaseSummary`, and the search box that filters what is counted.
The backend parses the search the same way as for the index. The response's
`matched` field is passed to `PublicationSearch` as its report.
