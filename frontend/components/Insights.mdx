# Insights

Counts for every publication in the database, or for the ones a search
matches. `Insights` is the content of the insights page at `/insights`, and
`InsightsHeading` is the page's subheader.

<Canvas of={InsightsStories.Everything} />

## Props

Both take an `insights` prop, the response of `GET /insights`. Most of its
counts are numbers of publications, so a book published twice counts twice.
`Insights` also takes `map`, the outlines of the world map, from `worldMap`.

Every figure and chart has an
[Info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs) that says what it counts.
The page has no introduction of its own, and the definitions it needs, such as
what a work is, are in the hints.

- **Figures** — see [Insight figures](?path=/docs/insights-insight-figures--docs).
- **Publications per year** — every year from the first to the last, split by
  the two countries with the most publications and everything else, drawn with
  [Insight columns](?path=/docs/insights-insight-columns--docs). Decades
  divisible by 20 are labelled under the chart, and the others too on wider
  screens.
- **Publications by decade** — every decade from the first to the last, split
  into first translations, retranslations and reissues, drawn with
  [Insight stacks](?path=/docs/insights-insight-stacks--docs). A reissue is a
  later publication of a translation, and a retranslation is the first
  publication of a work's second or later translation. The backend judges
  these over the whole database, so a search does not turn a reissue into a
  first translation.
- **Most translated authors** and **publishers** — up to ten names in each field
  with the most publications.
- **Authors first translated, by decade** — the authors the publications name,
  counted by the decade of their first publication in the database.
- **Translators with the most publications** — up to ten translators, each on
  an [Insight timeline](?path=/docs/insights-insight-timeline--docs) with a dot
  for each year they published in, sized by the publications that year.
- **Most frequent author–translator pairs** — up to ten pairs of an original
  author and a translator, by the publications they appear in together.
- **Works translated more than once** — a work is an original book, and a
  translation is one translated text of it by its translators. Each work is on
  a timeline with a dot for each translation, in the year it was first
  published, and its value counts translations, so a translation published
  twice counts once.
- **Publications by country** — every country, named in the reader's language,
  shaded on an [Insight map](?path=/docs/insights-insight-map--docs) and listed
  with its count beside it.

The year chart and the map take the full width. The other charts share a grid
of up to three columns, and the two timelines span two of them.

## Empty lists and empty results

The timeline of works translated more than once is left out when no work has
more than one translation. The other charts always have entries when there are
publications.

## InsightsHeading

`InsightsHeading` shows how many publications are counted, the links to the two
views from `DatabaseSummary`, and the search box that filters what is counted.
The backend parses the search the same way as for the index. The response's
`matched` field is passed to `PublicationSearch` as its report.
