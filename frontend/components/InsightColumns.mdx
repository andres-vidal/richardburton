# Insight columns

A titled column chart with one column for each year or decade, drawn in an
[Insight section](?path=/docs/insights-insight-section--docs). A column stacks
its series from the bottom, and its height is the sum of its counts as a
fraction of the largest sum, which is written at the top of the chart.

<Canvas of={InsightColumnsStories.Years} />

## Props

- `title` — the heading of the chart, and the accessible name of the section
  around it and of its table.
- `hint` — an optional explanation of what the chart shows, in an
  [Info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs) beside the title.
- `actions` — optional controls shown at the end of the title's row, such
  as a chart's settings.
- `heading` — the heading of the table's first column, such as "Year".
- `series` — the name of each series, in the order they are stacked from the
  bottom. With more than one, they are listed in an
  [Insight legend](?path=/docs/insights-insight-legend--docs).
- `columns` — every column to draw, in order. Each has `at`, its year or the
  first year of its decade, a `label`, and its `counts` in the order of
  `series`.
- `shares` — whether each column shows the share of each series in it.

## Labels under the columns

Years divisible by 20 are always labelled under the chart, and the other
decades only on screens at least as wide as the `md` breakpoint, where there is
room for them. `tickOf` in `modules/insights` decides which. A label under the
first or last column is aligned inwards, so it stays inside the chart.

## Hover

Hovering a column shows its label, its total, and its count in each series
where that count is above 0, with its share when `shares` is set.

## Accessibility

The drawing has `aria-hidden`. The section holds an
[Insight table](?path=/docs/insights-insight-table--docs) of the same counts,
which assistive technology reads instead.
