# Insight columns

A titled column chart with one column for each entry, such as each year. A
column stacks its series from the bottom, and its height is its total as a
fraction of the largest total, which is written at the top of the chart.

<Canvas of={InsightColumnsStories.Years} />

## Props

- `title` — the heading of the chart, and the accessible name of the section
  around it and of its table.
- `hint` — an optional explanation of what the chart shows, in an
  [Info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs) beside the title.
- `heading` — the heading of the table's first column, such as "Year".
- `series` — the name of each series, in the order they are stacked from the
  bottom. They are listed in an [Insight legend](?path=/docs/insights-insight-legend--docs).
- `columns` — the entries, each with a `key`, a `label`, its `counts` in the
  order of `series`, and an optional `tick`.

## Labels under the columns

A column marked with a `tick` has its label written under the chart. A `major`
label is always written, and a `minor` label only on screens at least as wide
as the `md` breakpoint, where there is room for it. A label under the first or
last column is aligned inwards, so it stays inside the chart.

<Canvas of={InsightColumnsStories.OneDecade} />

## Hover

Hovering a column shows its label, its total, and its count in each series
where that count is above 0. The play test of the first story checks the text
it shows for 2012.

## Accessibility

The drawing has `aria-hidden`. The section holds an
[Insight table](?path=/docs/insights-insight-table--docs) of the same counts,
which assistive technology reads instead.
