# Insight columns

A column chart with one column for each year or decade. A column stacks its
series from the bottom, and its height is the sum of its counts as a fraction
of the largest sum, which is written at the top of the chart.

<Canvas of={InsightColumnsStories.Years} />

## Props

- `title` — the caption of the table of counts, which is the title of the
  chart, and so the table's accessible name.
- `heading` — the heading of the table's first column, such as "Year".
- `series` — the name of each series, in the order they are stacked from the
  bottom. With more than one, they are listed in an
  [Insight legend](?path=/docs/insights-insight-legend--docs).
- `columns` — every column to draw, in order. Each has `at`, its year or the
  first year of its decade, a `label`, and its `counts` in the order of
  `series`.
- `shares` — whether each column shows the share of each series in it.

## Labels under the columns

The chart is drawn on an
[Insight axis](?path=/docs/insights-insight-axis--docs), which writes the
largest sum at the top and labels the columns underneath. Years divisible by 20
are always labelled, and the other decades only on screens at least as wide as
the `md` breakpoint, where there is room for them. A label under the first or
last column is aligned inwards, so it stays inside the chart.

## Hover

Hovering a column shows its label, its total, and its count in each series
where that count is above 0, with its share when `shares` is set.

## Accessibility

The drawing has `aria-hidden`. The component also renders an
[Insight table](?path=/docs/insights-insight-table--docs) of the same counts,
visually hidden, which assistive technology reads instead.
