# Text input

A single-line text field. It is controlled: it shows `value` and passes each
edit to `onChange` as a string.

<Canvas of={TextInputStories.Default} />

## Props

- `value` and `onChange` — the text, and the function that receives each edit.
- `label` — a label that floats above the field once it holds text or focus.
  Without one, the field shows its `placeholder` instead.
- `error` — marks the input invalid (`aria-invalid`) and tints the field red.
  A labelled field also adds the message to the label.
- `bordered` — outlines the field and sets it in `text-sm`, for a form. Without
  it the field is borderless and small, for a dense table cell.
- `fill` — stretches the field to its parent's height.
- `left` and `right` — content inside the field, before and after the input,
  such as the chips of a `Multicombobox`.
- `inputRef` — receives the input element. The component's `ref` receives the
  field around it.

Every other prop, such as `aria-label`, `disabled` or `onKeyDown`, goes to the
input element.

## Errors

A borderless field with an error is tinted red, and turns a stronger red while
it has focus. A bordered field draws a red border instead and keeps its white
background. In both, the text keeps enough contrast to be read.

## States

- **Default** — empty.
- **With value** — holding text.
- **Disabled** — greyed out, and takes no input.
