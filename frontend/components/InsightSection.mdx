# Insight section

A titled section of the insights page: a heading, an
[Info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs) that explains the section,
and the section's content. Every chart and list of insights is drawn inside one.

<Canvas of={InsightSectionStories.WithHint} />

## Props

- `title` — the heading's text. The section is labelled by the heading, so this
  is also the section's accessible name.
- `hint` — what the section shows. The hint's button is named "About" followed
  by the title.
- `children` — the section's content.

The hint sits beside the heading rather than inside it, so the heading's text,
and the accessible name of the section, is the title alone.

Without a `hint`, only the heading and the content are shown.
