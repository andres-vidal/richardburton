# Insight title

The heading of a chart or list of insights, followed by an
[Info hint](?path=/docs/components-info-hint--docs) that explains it.

<Canvas of={InsightTitleStories.WithHint} />

## Props

- `id` — the heading's id. The section around the chart is labelled by it.
- `title` — the heading's text.
- `hint` — what the chart shows. The hint's button is named "About" followed
  by the title.

The hint sits beside the heading rather than inside it, so the heading's text,
and the accessible name of the section it labels, is the title alone.

Without a `hint`, only the heading is shown.
