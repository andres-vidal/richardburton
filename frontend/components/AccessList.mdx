# People

Lists everyone with access, and what each may do. The offers of access still
waiting to be taken up are in
[Invitations](?path=/docs/access-invitations--docs).

## Roles

Each role admits everything the one before it admits:

- **Reader** — can browse the database, like anyone signed out.
- **Contributor** — can add and correct publications.
- **Administrator** — can do that, and decide who else may.

A row says what its role allows in words, rather than giving the role's name.

<Canvas of={AccessListStories.Default} />

## Your own row

Shown, but neither its role nor its access can be changed from it, so a slip
cannot lock you out. Another admin can change them. The server refuses the
change too, and also refuses to leave the platform without an admin.

<Canvas of={AccessListStories.YourOwnRow} />

## Changing a role

Picking a role from the [Role menu](?path=/docs/access-role-menu--docs) applies
it at once, with no save. The change takes effect on the person's next request,
because the server reads the role on every request.

<Canvas of={AccessListStories.ChangingSomeoneElsesRole} />

## Revoking

Revoking asks for confirmation first, and ends the person's sessions, so they
are signed out at once.
