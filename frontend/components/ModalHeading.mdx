# Modal heading

What a dialog is called, pinned to the top of it while its content scrolls.

A dialog that scrolls takes its heading out of view with everything else, and a
list of thirty things is exactly where the reader most needs to be told what
they are reading and how much of it there is.

<Canvas of={ModalHeadingStories.Default} />

## Props

- `heading` — what the dialog is called. Rendered as its `h1`.
- `subheading` — a line under it, pinned with it rather than scrolling away.
  Usually how much the dialog holds.
- `aside` — set against the heading at the end of the bar, for a dialog that is
  one of a queue.

## Where it can sit

It spans the dialog's padding with negative margins, so it has to sit directly
inside a `p-8` body. Outside one it will hang over the edges.

The background is translucent and blurred rather than opaque, so content reads
as passing underneath rather than stopping at an edge.

A heading longer than the bar is cut rather than allowed to wrap it open, since
a bar that grows as the reader moves through a queue is a bar that moves the
content under it.

## Not yet shared with `Article`

[Article](?path=/docs/layout-article--docs) carries the same pinned bar inline,
along with a watermark and an aside column. The two should be one; they are not
yet, because `Article` is what Learn More and Contact are built from and this
was not the change to move them.
