# Edit modal

A [modal](?path=/docs/components-modal--docs) for editing publications in the
enclosing publication store, which asks before it closes on an unsaved edit.
Closing it, with <kbd>Esc</kbd>, its close button or a press outside the
dialog, closes it at once while the store has no edit the database does not
have yet (see `hasUnsavedEdit` in `modules/publication/store`).

## Props

The props of `Modal`. `onClose` is called when the modal closes, at once or
after the discard is confirmed, and must drop the edit, for instance by
unmounting the store that holds it. A control that discards on purpose, such as
a form's Cancel button, calls it directly and asks nothing.

## Closing with an unsaved edit

With an unsaved edit, closing the modal leaves it open and a
[confirmation](?path=/docs/components-confirmation-modal--docs) asks whether to
discard the edit. Cancel, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or a press outside the confirmation
goes back to the modal with the edit as it was.
