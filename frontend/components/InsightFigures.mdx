# Insight figures

The headline figures of a set of publications: how many there are, how many
distinct works and names they hold, the years they span, and how many cite a
source. They open the insights page.

<Canvas of={InsightFiguresStories.Figures} />

## Props

A single `insights` prop, the description the backend returns for the
publications being counted. The figures are its `publications`, its `totals`,
its `years` and its `sourced`.

## Reading the figures

Counts are written in the reader's language, so a large one is grouped the way
they group numbers. Years are not quantities, so they are written without
grouping. With nothing counted, there are no years to span, and the span reads
as a dash.

<Canvas of={InsightFiguresStories.NothingCounted} />

## Accessibility

Each figure is a term and its value in a description list. The value is drawn
above its term but read after it, so a figure reads as "Publications, 428".
