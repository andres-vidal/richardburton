# Text area

A multi-line text field. It is controlled: it shows `value` and passes each
edit to `onChange` as a string.

<Canvas of={TextAreaStories.Default} />

## Props

- `value` and `onChange` — the text, and the function that receives each edit.
- `label` — a label that floats above the field once it holds text or focus.
  Without one, the field shows its `placeholder` instead.
- `error` — marks the field invalid (`aria-invalid`), tints it red, and adds
  the message to the label.
- `disabled` — greys the field out and stops it from taking input.

## Label

The labelled field sits on a grey surface, which takes an indigo tint while the
field has focus.

<Canvas of={TextAreaStories.FocusedWithLabel} />

## Error

A field with an error is tinted a light red. While it has focus it turns a
stronger red, and the text typed into it turns white, which keeps the text
readable. The labelled field does the same, and its label turns red and holds
the message.

<Canvas of={TextAreaStories.WithError} />

The focused field takes the stronger red with or without a label.

<Canvas of={TextAreaStories.FocusedWithError} />

## States

- **Default** — empty.
- **With value** — holding text.
- **With label** — the floating label.
- **Disabled** — greyed out and read-only.
- **With error**, **Focused with error**, and **Focused with label and error** —
  the red tints.
