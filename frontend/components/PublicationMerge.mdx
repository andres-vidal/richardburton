# Publication merge

Merges duplicates of a publication into it. The record the dialog is opened
from is the one that survives: it keeps its identity, its address and its own
fields, and gains the countries, publishers and sources the others hold. A
source that repeats one the survivor already has, with the same text in one
language and no text that differs in the other, is not added again, and the
kept source takes the text of the language it lacked. The merged record keeps
the notes shown in the dialog's notes field, in both languages. The records
merged in leave the database, recorded as merged rather than deleted.

## Usage

Controlled by the caller, like every dialog here: pair `isOpen` with
`useModal()`, close it on `onClose`, and read the record again on `onMerged`,
since the publication the caller is showing has changed.

```tsx
<PublicationMerge
  publication={publication}
  isOpen={mergeDialog.isOpen}
  onClose={mergeDialog.close}
  onMerged={() => {
    mergeDialog.close();
    router.refresh();
  }}
/>
```

## Props

- `publication` — the record the others are merged into, with its notes.
- `isOpen` and `onClose` — whether the dialog is open, and what closes it.
  Closing clears the search, the picks and the notes.
- `onMerged` — called once the merge has happened.
- `find` — looks up candidates for a term. It defaults to searching the
  database. The dialog calls it through `useLookup`, which waits for typing to
  pause, passes the term in lower case, and lets only the newest search fill
  the list.
- `read` — reads a stored publication in full, its notes included, by its id.
  It defaults to reading it from the database. The dialog reads each record
  when it is picked, since the records a search finds have no notes.

## Notes

When the survivor or a picked record has notes in either language, the Result
has a [notes editor](?path=/docs/publications-notes-editor--docs), with a tab
for each language. Each language is prefilled with the proposed notes in that
language: the survivor's notes, then each picked record's notes in the order
they were picked, without notes that are blank or that equal earlier ones. The
field is shown once every picked record is read. The merge sends the notes in
the field, in both languages, and the merged record keeps them. Blank notes are
sent as none.

<Description of={PublicationMergeStories.NotesProposed} />
<Canvas of={PublicationMergeStories.NotesProposed} />

The notes can be edited in the field, and drafted from the other language in a
browser that can translate. Until the notes of a language are edited, picking
or removing a record proposes them again. Once they are edited, the field keeps
the edited notes of that language, whatever is picked or removed, until the
dialog closes.

<Description of={PublicationMergeStories.EditingNotes} />
<Canvas of={PublicationMergeStories.EditingNotes} />

Notes longer than 10,000 characters in either language, counted as graphemes,
have an error under the field of that language, and Merge is disabled until
they are shorter.

<Description of={PublicationMergeStories.NotesTooLong} />
<Canvas of={PublicationMergeStories.NotesTooLong} />

When the server refuses the notes of a language, its error is under the field
of that language, and Merge is disabled until those notes are edited.

<Description of={PublicationMergeStories.NotesRefused} />
<Canvas of={PublicationMergeStories.NotesRefused} />

When no record has notes in either language, the Result has no notes field,
and the merge sends no notes.
