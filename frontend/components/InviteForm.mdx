# Invite someone

A form that offers a role to an email address. Submitting it either gives the
role to someone who already has an account, listed in
[People](?path=/docs/access-people--docs), or records an invitation, listed in
[Invitations](?path=/docs/access-invitations--docs).

<Canvas of={InviteFormStories.Default} />

## Any address

The form does not ask whether the person already has an account. The server
gives the role at once if they do, and records an invitation against the
address if they do not. A notification says which happened, "Access granted" or
"Invitation sent".

The form starts on **Contributor**, the role most often given.

**Reader** is offered too. Since an account is made only for someone invited, a
reader has an account with no privileges: they sign in, land on the pending
page, and can be given a role later without being invited again.

## States

- **Nothing typed** — there is nothing to send, so the button is disabled.
- **An address typed** — the button is enabled.
