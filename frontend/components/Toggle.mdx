# Toggle

A left-aligned [Button](?path=/docs/components-button--docs) that switches
something on or off. Checked, it is the primary variant with a check mark.
Unchecked, it is the outline variant with no icon. `aria-pressed` gives the
state to assistive technology.

<Description of={ToggleStories.Default} />
<Canvas of={ToggleStories.Default} />

## Props

- `label` — the button's label and accessible name.
- `checked` — whether it is on. The caller owns it and flips it in `onClick`.
- `onClick` — called on a click.
- `CheckedIcon` and `UncheckedIcon` — the icon for each state, a check mark and
  none by default. Give both the same icon for an icon-only toggle, with
  `labelSrOnly`.

Every other prop of `Button`, such as `width` or `labelSrOnly`, passes through.
