# Tooltip

Shows a message in a tooltip on its child while the child is hovered or
focused. The child must take a ref, and should be focusable, such as a button,
so the message can be reached from the keyboard.

<Description of={TooltipStories.Default} />
<Canvas of={TooltipStories.Default} />

## Props

- `message` — the text shown. When it is empty, the child renders alone, with
  no tooltip.
- `variant` — `error`, `warning` or `info`, each with its own colours and icon.
- `placement` — where the tooltip goes relative to its child, `top` by default.
- `boundary` — with `main`, the tooltip stays inside the page's `<main>`
  element.
- `absoluteCenter` — centres the tooltip horizontally in the viewport instead
  of on its child.

## TooltipProvider

`TooltipProvider` is the unstyled part of a tooltip, and is documented here
rather than on its own page. It shows any `content` next to its child, in a
portal with the `tooltip` role, while the child is hovered or focused. It closes
when the pointer leaves, when the child loses focus, and on Escape or a click
outside. It takes the same `placement`, `boundary` and `absoluteCenter` props.
