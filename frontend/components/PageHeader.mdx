# Page header

The heading of a page: a left-aligned title, the page's `<h1>`, with an
optional muted description under it. It goes in a `Layout` subheader, under the
breadcrumb, and lines up with it.

## Props

- `title` — the page title, rendered as the page's `<h1>`.
- `description` — an optional supporting line under the title (a short sentence of
  context).

<Canvas of={PageHeaderStories.Default} />

## States

- **Default** — a title with a description.
- **Title only** — without a description, when the title says enough.
