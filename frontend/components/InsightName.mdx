# Insight name

The name of a group on a chart: its label, followed by its detail in grey after
a middle dot, as in "Dom Casmurro · Machado de Assis".

<Canvas of={InsightNameStories.WithDetail} />

## Props

- `label` — the group's name, such as a work's title or an author.
- `detail` — optional extra text after the label, such as a work's authors or
  the translator of an author–translator pair.

The component renders only text and the span around the detail. The element it
is placed in sets the label's size and colour.

## Without a detail

Without a detail, the name is the label alone.
