# Admin menu

Renders a card for each admin tool, linking to its route with an icon, a title
and a one-line description. The icon is hidden from assistive technology, since
the title says the same.

<Description of={AdminMenuStories.Default} />
<Canvas of={AdminMenuStories.Default} />

## The tools

The menu takes no props. The tools are the `ACTIONS` list in the component, and
a new tool is added there with its `href`, its `Icon`, the `key` of its
`…Title` and `…Description` messages in the `admin` namespace, and its `role`:

- **Add publications** → `/admin/publications/documents`
- **Backfill sources** → `/admin/publications/sources`
- **Review duplicates** → `/admin/publications/duplicates`
- **History** → `/admin/publications/history`
- **Deleted publications** → `/admin/publications/deleted`
- **Names** → `/admin/vocabulary`
- **Access** → `/admin/users`

## Who sees what

Each tool names the least role that may reach it, which is the role its route
asks for, and the menu shows only the tools the signed-in role reaches, so no
card leads to a page that would turn the reader away.
