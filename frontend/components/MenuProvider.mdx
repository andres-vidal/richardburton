# Menu provider

A list of options that floats under its child, the trigger, in a portal while
`isOpen`. The arrow keys move a highlight through the options while focus stays
on the trigger, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> or a press outside closes the list. Clicking
an option closes the list and calls `onSelect` with it.

The provider does not open the list itself: its caller keeps `isOpen` and the
highlighted `activeIndex`, and opens the list from its trigger, such as on a
click or on typing.

## Props

- `children` — the trigger, which must take a ref. Pairing it with the list
  gives it the `combobox` role.
- `options` — strings, or `{ id, label, description? }` objects. A
  `description` is a quieter second line under the label, for options whose
  labels alone do not tell them apart.
- `isOpen` and `setIsOpen` — whether the list is shown.
- `activeIndex` and `setActiveIndex` — the highlighted option.
- `onSelect` — called with the option clicked.
- `bordered` — draws the list outlined, to match a bordered input.
- `emptyMessage` — shown in place of the list when there are no options.

Pressing on an option does not move focus off the trigger. When the list closes,
focus returns to the trigger, which already has it, so a trigger that opens the
list on focus does not open it again.

## Menu and MenuItem

`Menu` and `MenuItem` are the parts the provider renders the list with, and are
documented here rather than on pages of their own.

`Menu` is the scrollable list, an `<ol>` with the `listbox` role. Its props are
those of an `<ol>` other than `className`, plus `bordered`.

`MenuItem` is one option, an `<li>` with the `option` role. It does not take
focus. `selected` highlights it and sets `aria-selected`, and its other props
are those of an `<li>` other than `className`.
