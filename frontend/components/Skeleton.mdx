# Skeleton

The pieces that a loading placeholder is built from. `Skeleton` is the status
around the placeholder, `SkeletonBar` is one grey shape, and `skeletonRow`
gives a placeholder row its index. The
[table skeleton](?path=/docs/publications-index-table-skeleton--docs) and the
[list skeleton](?path=/docs/publications-index-list-skeleton--docs) are built
from them.

## Appearing late

`Skeleton` has the `animate-skeleton-appear` animation from
`styles/globals.css`. The animation has a 150ms delay and the `both` fill mode,
so the status is transparent for its first 150ms and then fades in over
200ms. A load that finishes within 150ms therefore never shows the skeleton.

## Fading out

`Skeleton` has a `mask-b-from-50%` mask, a linear gradient that is opaque in
the top half and fades to transparent at the bottom edge. The rows in the
lower half get paler towards the bottom, and the last row is nearly
transparent.

## The wave

`SkeletonBar` has the `animate-skeleton-wave` animation, which pulses its
opacity every 2s. The animation's delay is `calc(var(--row, 0) * 90ms)`, so a
shape starts its pulse 90ms later for each step of the `--row` custom property.
A placeholder row sets `--row` to its index with `skeletonRow`, and the pulse
moves down the rows.

The animation is under the `motion-safe` variant. When the browser asks for
reduced motion, the shapes are still. The appearance is a fade without
movement, so it plays either way.

## Props

`Skeleton` takes `children`, the placeholder, which it hides from assistive
technology.

`SkeletonBar` takes:

- `shape` — `line` for a line of text, `pill` for a tag that holds one name,
  and `button` for a small button. Defaults to `line`.
- `length` — how long the shape is. A line takes a share of the width it is
  in: `short` a third, `medium` a half, `long` three quarters and `full` all of
  it. A pill or a button has a fixed length: `short` 3rem, `medium` 5rem and
  `long` 7rem, and `full` takes all of the width. Defaults to `full`. In a
  flex container that is too narrow for its shapes, a shape shrinks below its
  length.
- `muted` — whether the shape is paler, for secondary text.

`skeletonRow(index)` returns a `style` that sets `--row` to `index`.

## Accessibility

The status has the `status` role and is named "Loading", so assistive
technology announces that something is loading. The placeholder is hidden from
it.
