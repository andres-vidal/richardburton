# Article

Renders a long-form article with its own `p-8` padding: a heading in a bar
pinned to the top while the content scrolls, then the content, with an aside
beside it when there is one. The app's logo sits faintly behind the text. It
fills a [Modal](?path=/docs/components-modal--docs) that has no `heading` of its
own.

<Description of={ArticleStories.Default} />
<Canvas of={ArticleStories.Default} />

## Props

- `heading` — the article's `h1`, in the pinned bar. It can hold controls beside
  the text, such as a copy-link button.
- `subheading` — a line under the heading, pinned with it.
- `content` — the article's text.
- `aside` — a column beside the content from the `sm` breakpoint up, and under
  it below.
- `asideHeading` — the aside's heading. From the `sm` breakpoint up it sits in
  the pinned bar above the aside, and below it heads the aside itself.

## With an aside

The content takes seven twelfths of the width and the aside the rest. Drag the
box's corner below the `sm` breakpoint to see the aside move under the content.
