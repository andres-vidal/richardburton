# Insight legend

The legend of a chart. Each entry is listed with a swatch of its colour, in
order.

<Canvas of={InsightLegendStories.ThreeSeries} />

## Props

- `series` — the name of each entry. Without `shaded`, the first takes the main
  colour, the second a colour that stands out from it, and the third a muted
  one.
- `shaded` — whether the entries are bands of shades, from the lightest, rather
  than series.

## Colours

The colours of the series come from the `bg-series` utility in
`styles/globals.css`, which picks a fill from the element's `data-series`
attribute. The charts use the same utility, so a series has the same colour in
the legend and in the chart.

A chart with fewer series uses the first colours.

<Canvas of={InsightLegendStories.TwoSeries} />

## Shades

With `shaded`, each swatch has `data-shade` instead, from 1 for the first band
to 4 for the last, and takes the band's colour from the `data-shade` rules in
`styles/globals.css`. Shaded charts set the same attribute on their marks.
