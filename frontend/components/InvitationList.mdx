# Invitations

Lists the invitations, each an offer of a role to an address, waiting and taken
up. The people who already have access are in
[People](?path=/docs/access-people--docs).

## Why an invitation exists at all

An admin cannot grant a role to someone who has never signed in: the identity
provider mints the account, and only on that first sign-in. So the offer waits
on the address instead, and signing in with it redeems the offer.

For the same reason no confirmation link is sent. The provider vouches for the
address at sign-in, and a link mailed to the same address would prove nothing
more.

Someone who already has an account is given the role outright, and no invitation
is recorded; there is nothing left to wait for.

<Canvas of={InvitationListStories.Default} />

## States

- **waiting** — nobody has signed in with that address yet. It can be sent again
  or withdrawn.
- **taken up** — someone signed in and the role was applied. It stays listed as
  the record of how they came to have it, and offers no actions.

An invitation is redeemed once. Withdrawing one that is waiting removes the
offer; the address can be invited again afterwards.

## When the mail fails

The offer is recorded before the mail is sent, so a failed mail does not cost
someone their role. The invitation stands and is reported as unsent, and
**Send again** retries it.
