# Insight chart

A chart of the counts `GET /insights` returns, in the form its description
names, or a table of the same counts.

<Canvas of={InsightChartStories.PerYear} />

## Props

- `description` — the chart, as the page's address describes it (see
  `modules/chart`).
- `drawn` — its counts: the series, the groups with their counts, and how many
  groups `top` left out.
- `view` — `"chart"` to draw the chart, or `"table"` to show its counts in an
  [Insight table](?path=/docs/insights-insight-table--docs).
- `actions` — controls shown at the end of the chart's title row, whatever
  the form.

## Forms

Each form is drawn by its own component:

- columns, and shares over time —
  [Insight columns](?path=/docs/insights-insight-columns--docs);
- lines, stacked areas and running totals —
  [Insight lines](?path=/docs/insights-insight-lines--docs);
- bars — [Insight bars](?path=/docs/insights-insight-bars--docs) for one series,
  and [Insight stacks](?path=/docs/insights-insight-stacks--docs) for several,
  which also draws shares of groups that are not years or decades;
- heat tables — [Insight heat](?path=/docs/insights-insight-heat--docs);
- timelines — [Insight timeline](?path=/docs/insights-insight-timeline--docs);
- maps — [Insight map](?path=/docs/insights-insight-map--docs).

## Names

The component names each group from its key. Years are written as numbers and
decades as "1950s". Kinds are named in the reader's language and countries by
their names. A work shows its authors as the detail, and a pair of an original
author and a translator shows the translator. The series are the kinds, the
leading countries and "Elsewhere", or the name of what is counted when there is
one series.
