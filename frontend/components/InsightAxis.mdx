# Insight axis

The axes of a chart over years or decades. The component renders a box for the
plot, with a dashed line along its top edge and the value at the top written
above it, and labels the years under the box.

<Canvas of={InsightAxisStories.Years} />

## Props

- `ats` — the year of each slot under the plot, or the first year of its
  decade, in order.
- `top` — the text at the top of the plot, such as the largest count drawn.
- `spaced` — whether the slots are one pixel apart, rather than side by side.
- `children` — the plot. The box is `h-48` tall, as wide as the axis, and
  positioned, so a plot can fill it with `absolute inset-0`.

## Labels

Each year has an equal slot under the plot, and its label is centred in its
slot. Years divisible by 20 are always labelled, and the other decades only on
screens at least as wide as the `md` breakpoint, where there is room for them.
`tickOf` in `modules/insights` decides which. A label in the first or last slot
is aligned inwards, so it stays inside the chart.

## Accessibility

The axes and the plot have `aria-hidden`. A chart drawn on the axis needs its
counts in another form for assistive technology, such as an
[Insight table](?path=/docs/insights-insight-table--docs).
