    RichardBurton.Publication.Index.Keywords (richard_burton v0.1.0)

    Maps the words someone typed onto the words the index actually contains.

    search_keywords is a materialized view holding every distinct word in the search documents. Terms are matched against it one word at a time, because neither kind of match works on anything longer: a prefix is the beginning of a single word, and trigram similarity between a word and a whole phrase drops towards zero as the phrase gets longer.

    A word's standing is how it matched the indexed words, and which ones it matched. It is one of two tuples:

    • {:prefix, words} — the indexed words that start with the word as typed. A word stands this way when at least one indexed word starts with it.
    • {:fuzzy, words} — the indexed words that resemble the word, most similar first. A word stands this way when no indexed word starts with it, and words is empty when none resembles it either.
