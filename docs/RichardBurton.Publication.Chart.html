    RichardBurton.Publication.Chart (richard_burton v0.1.0)

    Describes a chart of publications, and counts what the chart shows.

    A chart counts items in groups. An item is what count names, and a group is what by names. A group counts each item once, however many of its publications hold it. A chart can also split each group into series, or into steps of time. parse/1 reads a chart from request parameters, and draw/2 counts it.

    A chart has these fields:

    • count — the items: :publications, :works, :translations, :original_authors, :translators or :publishers. Works and translations are those RichardBurton.Publication.Insights defines.
    • by — the groups: :year, :decade, :kind, :country, :original_author, :translator, :publisher, :work, or :pair, which is an original author and a translator together. A publication is in one year, decade, kind and work, and in one group for each country, name or pair it holds. A decade is named by its first year.
    • split — the series: nil for none, :kind, or :country. A publication is in one series. With :country, the series are the leading countries and one more for every other country, and a publication is in the first leading country it holds. Items other than publications can be in several series of a group, so the counts of a group's series can add up to more than its total.
    • over — the steps: nil for none, :year or :decade. It needs a by that is not a year or a decade, and no split.
    • first — when true, each item is counted at its first appearance only: in the group and step of its earliest publication in the whole index, by year and then by id, and in that publication's series. Within a group of names, works, pairs or countries, an item's earliest publication is the earliest in that group. Only the items the counted publications hold are counted, even when their first appearance is outside them. It needs by or over to be a year or a decade.
    • top — how many groups to keep, or nil to keep them all.
    • least — the fewest items a group needs to be kept, or nil to keep groups of any size.

    Groups by year or decade are listed by time, and groups by kind in the order of RichardBurton.Publication.Kind.all/0. They cannot take top or least. Every other group is ranked, with the most items first. Groups with as many items are sorted by their number of publications, most first, and then by their names.

    Terms used here:

    • kind — whether a publication is a first translation, a retranslation or a reissue, as RichardBurton.Publication.Kind defines them. Kinds are judged over the whole index, so a search does not turn a reissue into a first translation.
    • leading countries — the two countries with the most publications among those counted. Countries with the same count are sorted by code.

    draw(base, chart)

    @spec draw(Ecto.Queryable.t(), t()) :: map()

    Counts chart over the publications base selects, a query of FlatPublication.

    Returns a map with these keys:

    • series — the series, in order. They are [nil] without a split, the kinds with split: :kind, and the codes of the leading countries followed by nil for every other country with split: :country.
    • groups — the groups, in the order the module doc gives. Groups by year or decade run from the earliest to the latest, including those with no items, and groups by kind include all three kinds. Each group has key, total, the number of distinct items in the group, and counts, the number in each series, in the order of series. With over, it also has steps.
    • hidden — the number of ranked groups that top leaves out.

    A group's key is a year or a decade as an integer, a kind, a country code or a name as a string, %{title: title, authors: authors} for a work, and %{author: author, translator: translator} for a pair.

    Each step has at, its year or decade, total, the number of distinct items in the group in that step, and items, the names of those items. An item's names are a publication's title, a work's original title, a translation's translators, or the one name or country code it is. Items with the same names are listed once. A group's steps are only those with items, earliest first.
