    RichardBurton.Publication.Kind (richard_burton v0.1.0)

    Tells whether a publication is a first translation of its work, a retranslation or a reissue, and which earlier publications it follows.

    Works and translations are those RichardBurton.Publication.Insights defines. Publications are compared by year only. Two publications of the same year count as published at the same time, whatever order they were entered in. Terms used here:

    • first year — the year of the earliest publication of a work, or of a translation.
    • first edition — a publication of a translation in that translation's first year. A translation published several times in its first year has several first editions.
    • kind — a publication is a "reissue" when it was published in a later year than its translation's first year. Otherwise it is a first edition, which is a "first_translation" when its translation's first year is the work's first year, and a "retranslation" when its translation's first year is later. A work has as many first translations as it has publications in its first year.
    • follows — the earlier publications that a retranslation or a reissue comes after. A reissue follows every first edition of its translation. A retranslation follows every publication of its work in the work's first year, which are the work's first translations. A first translation follows none.

    A publication's kind depends on the publications it is compared with. ranked/1 compares the publications of a query with each other, and of/1 compares one publication with every publication of its work that is not deleted.

    Summary

    Functions

    all()

    Returns the kinds: "first_translation", "retranslation" and "reissue", in that order.

    of(publication)

    Returns the kind of publication and the publications it follows, as a map with kind and follows. follows lists publications preloaded as RichardBurton.Publication.with_ids/1 preloads them, in ascending order of id, and is empty for a first translation.

    ranked(source)

    Returns a query with one row for each publication of source. Each row has the publication's id, its kind, and follows, the ids of the publications it follows in ascending order. follows is an empty list for a first translation.

    all()

    of(publication)

    @spec of(RichardBurton.Publication.t()) :: %{
  kind: String.t(),
  follows: [RichardBurton.Publication.t()]
}

    Returns the kind of publication and the publications it follows, as a map with kind and follows. follows lists publications preloaded as RichardBurton.Publication.with_ids/1 preloads them, in ascending order of id, and is empty for a first translation.

    publication is compared with every publication of its work that is not deleted. They are read from the publications table rather than from the index, so a publication written since the index was last refreshed is counted. publication must not be deleted, and needs its translated book loaded.

    ranked(source)

    @spec ranked(Ecto.Queryable.t()) :: Ecto.Query.t()

    Returns a query with one row for each publication of source. Each row has the publication's id, its kind, and follows, the ids of the publications it follows in ascending order. follows is an empty list for a first translation.

    source is a queryable of publications whose rows have an id and a year, such as RichardBurton.FlatPublication or a query of RichardBurton.Publication. Each publication is compared only with the other publications of source.
