    RichardBurton.Invitation (richard_burton v0.1.0)

    A role held for an email address until whoever owns it signs in.

    An admin cannot grant a role to someone who has never signed in, because the identity provider mints the subject id only on a first sign-in. So the role waits on the address instead, and the first sign-in with that address redeems it. The provider vouching for the address is the confirmation, so no separate confirmation link is sent. RichardBurton.Auth.Claims refuses a token that does not carry a verified email, which is what this rests on.

    Someone who already has an account is given the role outright, and no invitation is recorded.

    An invitation is pending until it is redeemed, and accepted after. An address has at most one pending invitation.

    admit(subject_id, email)

    Admits a signed-in identity when it is one this platform knows.

    An account is made only for someone who was invited. Anyone may hold a Google account, so a sign-in that created one regardless would let whoever tries decide who has an account, and revoking an account would mean nothing, since signing in again would make another. The new account takes up the pending invitation in the same transaction, so it starts with the offered role.

    Someone who already has an account is admitted on the strength of it. If a pending invitation is also waiting for their address, they take it up, and their role is raised to the offered one when the offered one is higher. An invitation never lowers a role.

    Returns {:ok, user}, :not_invited for someone with neither an account nor a pending invitation, or {:error, changeset} when the account cannot be made.

    invite(attrs, invited_by \\ nil)

    Offers the role in attrs["role"] to the address in attrs["email"], on behalf of invited_by, and mails the offer.

    Someone who already has an account gets the role now, and no invitation is recorded. Returns {:ok, {:granted, user}}, or the errors of RichardBurton.User.set_role/3.

    Otherwise it records a pending invitation and mails it. Returns {:ok, {:invited, invitation}}, or {:ok, {:unsent, invitation}} when the mail could not be sent. The invitation is recorded first, so a failure to send does not lose it, and it can be sent again with resend/1.

    Returns {:error, :invalid_role} when the role is missing or is not a role, {:error, :pending} when the address already has a pending invitation, and {:error, changeset} when the address is not one.

    Mails a pending invitation again. Returns {:ok, invitation}, {:error, :unsent} when the mail could not be sent, or {:error, :accepted} for an invitation already accepted.
